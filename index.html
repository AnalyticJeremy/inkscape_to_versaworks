Before you begin

Prepare the cut path in Inkscape

  1. 1
    Use a vector stroke

    Keep the cut path as a thin stroked path, not a raster image.

  2. 2
    Choose the guide color

    Use magenta #FF00FF for contour cuts or red #FF0000 for full perf cuts.

  3. 3
    Export as PDF

    Keep vectors intact, then select that PDF in this app.

Always verify the result. Confirm the cut-path preview and cutting settings in VersaWorks before starting a job.
